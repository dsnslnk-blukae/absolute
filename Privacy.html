Absol Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

What Absol is

Absol is an end-to-end encrypted messaging app. Your messages are encrypted on your device before they are sent, so the server stores only scrambled text.

What we collect

  • Your Google account email and name, used only to create and verify your account.
  • Your username, your public encryption key, and optional profile details you add (display name, bio, photo, email, phone number).
  • Encrypted messages, encrypted files and voice messages, and encrypted call signals. We cannot read them.
  • Settings such as theme, blocked contacts and notification choices.

Your encryption key and Google Drive

If you choose to back up your key, Absol saves it in a hidden app folder in your own Google Drive. Absol asks only for access to that folder (the drive.appdata permission), cannot see your other Drive files, and does not keep a copy of your key on our server.

How we use data

Only to run the app: delivering messages, showing your profile to people you chat with, and keeping your account secure. We do not sell your data or show ads.

Sharing

Your data is stored with our hosting provider, Supabase. We do not share it with anyone else, except where the law requires.

Deleting your data

Open Settings, then Account and security, then Delete account. This permanently deletes your profile, messages and files. You can also remove Absol's access in your Google Account settings.
